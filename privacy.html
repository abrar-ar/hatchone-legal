HatchOne — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-12 Owner: M. Abrar Rahman ("HatchOne", "we", "us") Contact: hatchone.app@gmail.com

This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — when you register or log in, our authentication provider (Supabase) processes your email address and an encrypted password credential, plus session tokens stored on your device to keep you signed in. You may also use the app as a guest, without an account.
  • Project inputs you enter — plot area, road width, occupancy, and similar figures you type to get calculations. These are stored locally on your device to save your projects; they are your working data.
  • Feedback you submit — if you use "Report a bug" or join the Pro waitlist, we receive the message and any email you optionally provide, relayed to our team.
  • Plot lookups — if you look up a plot, the plot identifier/search term is sent to our server (Cloudflare Worker) to return the matching record. We do not require personal information for lookups.

2. What we do NOT collect

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your project inputs for advertising. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • To provide calculations and save your projects on your device.
  • To respond to feedback and manage the Pro waitlist.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Service.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication and account storage.
  • Cloudflare — hosting of our plot-lookup worker/database.
  • Telegram — internal relay used to deliver your feedback messages to our team.
  • Expo / EAS — app delivery and over-the-air updates.

Each processes data on our behalf under their own terms; we share only what is necessary for the function described.

5. Data retention

Account data persists until you request deletion. Local project data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Feedback messages are retained as needed to respond and improve the Service.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by contacting hatchone.app@gmail.com. You can use the app as a guest to avoid creating an account.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (including transport encryption and provider-side access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside Bangladesh. By using the Service you consent to such transfers as necessary to provide it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Effective date" will change accordingly.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: hatchone.app@gmail.com
