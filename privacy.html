HatchOne — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-15 Owner: M. Abrar Rahman ("HatchOne", "we", "us") Contact: hatchone.app@gmail.com

This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — when you register or log in, our authentication provider (Supabase) processes your email address and a securely hashed password credential, plus session tokens stored on your device to keep you signed in. Native apps store those tokens in the operating system's secure credential storage; the web app uses browser storage. An account is required to use the app — there is no anonymous mode.
  • Project inputs you enter — plot area, road width, occupancy, and similar figures you type to get calculations. These are stored locally on your device to save your projects; they are your working data.
  • Feedback you submit — if you use "Report a bug", we receive the message and any email you optionally provide, relayed to our team.
  • Updates list details — if you choose to sign up for HatchOne updates, we collect the name, email address and professional category you provide, and the company/practice name and phone or WhatsApp number if you choose to add them (both optional). These are stored in our Supabase database and a notification is relayed to our team. Signing up is entirely optional; every calculation in the app works without it.
  • Plot lookups — if you look up a plot, the plot identifier/search term is sent to our server (Cloudflare Worker) to return the matching record. The search term itself contains no personal information. Lookups require a signed-in account, and we count how many plots you open so we can apply fair-use limits.

2. What we do NOT collect

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your project inputs for advertising. We do not knowingly collect data from children. We do not track your use of the app for analytics or advertising: the "Explore Hatch Studio" link simply opens hatchbangladesh.com in your browser, and we do not record who tapped it.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • To provide calculations and save your projects on your device.
  • To respond to feedback and to send product updates to people who asked for them.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Service.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication and account storage.
  • Cloudflare — hosting of our plot-lookup worker/database.
  • Telegram — internal relay used to deliver your feedback messages to our team.
  • Expo / EAS — app delivery and over-the-air updates.

Each processes data on our behalf under their own terms; we share only what is necessary for the function described.

5. Data retention

Account data persists until you delete your account in the app or request deletion. Local project data remains on your device until you delete the relevant project, delete your account in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Feedback messages are retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond, investigate issues, and improve the Service. Because feedback can be submitted without an account and is relayed separately, deleting your account does not automatically delete previously submitted feedback. Updates-list entries are kept until you ask us to remove you. That list is separate from your account, so deleting your account does not remove your entry — email us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

You can permanently delete your account using Delete account in the app's overflow menu. Successful deletion removes your authentication account and session and clears locally saved projects and project preferences from that device. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of account data by contacting hatchone.app@gmail.com.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (including transport encryption and provider-side access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside Bangladesh. By using the Service you consent to such transfers as necessary to provide it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Effective date" will change accordingly.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: hatchone.app@gmail.com
